Software Engineering

Software engineering is a disciplined approach to creating software by following organized methods rather than simply writing code. It emphasizes planning and structure to ensure software is dependable, maintainable, and able to meet user expectations. This approach is important because modern software projects often involve complex requirements, tight schedules, and teamwork, and having a structured process helps reduce errors, inefficiency, and confusion.

The software engineering process usually involves identifying requirements, designing the system, writing the code, testing to find and fix issues, releasing the software to users, and maintaining it after deployment. Software quality is measured not only by whether it functions correctly, but also by factors such as efficiency, security, and ease of use.
